### Integrate Data

Sync ClickUp with tools like Slack to push updates whenever a new
task is created or connect external systems like Salesforce for
seamless data flow and team alignment.

### Automate Workflows

Trigger actions or automate repetitive tasks effortlessly. Assign
leads in Salesforce, update task statuses in ClickUp, or streamline
processes across platforms.
